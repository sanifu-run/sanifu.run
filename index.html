Why pay when I can learn with AI and free tutorials?

Independent learning can work well. Choose Sanifu when you want someone experienced to help scope your own project, review the work with you, and explain a decision as you make it. The free call helps you decide whether that personal feedback is worth the workshop fee.

What can we accomplish in four hours?

We agree on one useful slice after the scope call: for example, one create-and-view workflow in a request tracker, or a baseline and evaluation for a suitable ML task. The aim is a checked result you understand. A complete production application, every integration, and a particular model accuracy are outside a blanket four-hour promise.

What happens after the workshop?

You keep your code and accounts, with written next steps. The pilot includes one batch of questions within seven days, up to 30 minutes of David’s time, plus ongoing community access with onboarding confirmed for your workshop. Further paid sessions are scoped separately. Any model or hosting costs are agreed before use.

Do I need programming experience or a finished idea?

No programming experience is required; you should be comfortable using a computer and willing to build hands-on. Bring an idea, a business problem, or an existing AI-built prototype. David can help you choose a small project if you are unsure where to begin. You do not need a client.

Can you help if I’m already vibe coding?

Yes. Bring the prototype and the part you do not understand or trust. Together, you can trace a bug, review a change, test a critical path, or plan the next feature. The scope call selects one useful focus; a workshop is not a comprehensive security audit or a promise that the whole app is ready for customers.

Is this a course for getting a developer job?

Sanifu’s initial focus is entrepreneurs and independent builders developing their own software. It does not provide a job-placement program, interview preparation, or promises of employment or income. An optional client project can be used for practice; you retain that relationship and any fees.
