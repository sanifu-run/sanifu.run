What happens in a workshop?

  1. 1. Choose a finish line

    On the free call, we discuss your priorities, tools and who will use the setup. If a workshop is a fit, you’ll receive a written scope and quote describing the agreed deliverables and what you’ll practise.

  2. 2. Build and test together

    Bring the relevant accounts, information and questions. David works alongside you as you set up the agreed improvement, try it, review the results and make changes. Longer programmes can be split across sessions.

  3. 3. Practise running it

    Before the workshop ends, practise the agreed operating and maintenance steps. You’ll leave with guidance and next steps suited to the scope, so you know how to continue improving your setup.

Questions before you book

Do I need technical experience?

Bring your knowledge of your business and the people who will use the setup. The workshop is hands-on: David works alongside you and explains the steps as you practise. We’ll discuss your experience and the project’s fit on the free call.

Can I operate everything by conversation?

Conversation is one way to ask for work, review output and refine instructions. Some settings and checks need attention directly in the selected tools; we’ll cover the relevant ones in your workshop.

What costs are separate?

Workshop prices cover the agreed live working time. Domain renewals, phone and AI usage, and paid software are separate. If free website hosting suits your project, we can help set it up and show you how to manage it; you may also keep a suitable existing host. We’ll identify relevant costs before the workshop.

Will this guarantee search rankings or AI citations?

No. We can improve agreed pages and show you how to maintain them, but search and answer engines determine their own results.
