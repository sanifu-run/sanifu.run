Your project is
the curriculum.

Sanifu is a hands-on workshop shaped around what you want to build. Comfortable using a computer? You can start without programming experience. You do the building, with David beside you.

  1. Bring a real problem

    Your own product or a client’s project. Start with a free 20–30 minute call to discuss your goals, experience, and what’s practical.

  2. Build one useful part

    Agree on a focused outcome and a tailored agenda. Learn to plan, direct AI tools, build, and test a working part of your product in a private four-hour workshop.

  3. Keep building

    Leave with your work, reusable techniques, and next steps. Larger products may need more sessions. Shorter paid appointments and full workshops are available by agreement.

Have a paying client?
