Questions before you book

Do I need technical experience?

Bring your knowledge of your business and the people who will use the setup. David works alongside you and explains the steps as you practise. Experience and fit are discussed on the free call.

Can I operate everything by conversation?

Conversation is one way to ask for work, review output, and refine instructions. Some settings and checks need attention directly in the selected tools. Those are covered in your workshop.

What costs are separate?

Workshop prices cover the agreed live working time. Domain renewals, phone and AI usage, and paid software are separate. If free website hosting suits your project, we can help set it up and show you how to manage it. You may also keep a suitable existing host. Relevant costs are identified before the workshop.

Will this guarantee search rankings or AI citations?

No. We can improve agreed pages and show you how to maintain them, but search and answer engines determine their own results.
