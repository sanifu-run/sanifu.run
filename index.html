The show

Learn from people doing the work.

Every industry has its own realities. Sanifu will talk with people making decisions inside businesses, so others in their field can hear how peers approach the same kinds of challenges. The conversations also help people who build technology hear directly from the businesses they want to serve.

We will explore what guests use AI for today, what they are considering, what has worked, and what remains difficult. If AI is not part of the picture yet, that is a useful story too.

What we talk about

The business comes first.

The work

How the business operates, where people spend time, and which decisions matter.

The challenges

The handoffs, recurring problems, and trade-offs that are easy to miss from the outside.

The possibilities

Where AI helps, where it falls short, and what guests want to understand next.

Your host

Curiosity, grounded in real businesses.

Sanifu is hosted by David Ndungu, a software engineer who wants to understand businesses by listening to the people who run them.

Sanifu aims to bring business owners and decision-makers into conversation with a wider audience, including the people building technology. It is a place to share experiences, learn from peers, and speak candidly about the tools and approaches that work—or do not work.
