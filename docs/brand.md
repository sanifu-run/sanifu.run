## Offering — founder direction, 2026-10-07

Build your AI software idea with a senior engineer beside you.

Lead with entrepreneurs who have an idea or business problem but do not know where to start, and vibe coders who have a prototype but want help deciding what works and what to do next. Explain vibe coding as building with AI coding tools when needed. The value is David’s engineering judgment applied to the learner’s work: scope, debugging, review, meaningful checks, and a continuation plan. Confidence is a learning aim supported by practice, not a guaranteed feeling or proof of production readiness.

Use one-to-one coaching and private workshop language together: four live hours, preparation and existing bounded follow-up. Preserve the approved commercial policy. Lead with the learner’s own product; an optional client project and specialist ML are secondary. Do not lead with earnings, employability or a generic learn-to-code course. Do not promise a complete startup, validated demand, a security audit or a production launch in four hours.

Examples may include a support-answer prototype using sample FAQs, a feedback sorter, or a draft follow-up helper with human approval. These are candidate slices requiring fit and scope, not available products or promised integrations.
