Updated 1 October 2026. This page describes the intended Sanifu intake and the local manual option. AI intake is available only when the website shows a connected assistant.

What you share

Use the intake for your learning goals, experience and practical constraints. Please don’t include passwords, API keys, private client records, financial account details or sensitive personal information.

At the start of connected chat, we ask for your name and email so David can follow up about your workshop interests. Sharing is optional. Sanifu will never sell your information or spam you. Contact details you type in chat are processed by the AI and saved with the conversation, including the transcript copies emailed to David, under the retention terms below. This does not sign you up for a newsletter.

AI intake and saved conversations

When connected, your messages and AI replies are sent through the Experiential gateway to its configured inference provider and saved in AWS DynamoDB for David to review. A random token stored in your browser lets you return to the conversation. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to open it. Clearing browser storage removes your recovery token, not the server copy.

AWS transcripts expire 30 days after their last update. Expired chats are immediately hidden by the application; DynamoDB physically deletes them later using its TTL process. Point-in-time backups are disabled. You can delete a saved conversation in chat when connected; David can also delete it.

Full transcript copies are emailed to David after a chat has been idle for at least five minutes and again when it changes. Website deletion or expiry does not delete those email copies. Experiential prompt capture is enabled without a fixed expiry. The 30-day limit applies only to AWS transcripts, not email copies or records retained by the gateway and its inference provider. Do not submit sensitive information.

Your learner brief

You can review, edit and approve the summary. Approval confirms that it accurately represents your needs. It does not purchase a workshop, approve a final agenda, enroll you in marketing, or authorize a payment.

Offline chat and background video

When the AI is offline, typed messages and prepared answers stay in the current page and disappear when you reload. They are not submitted or stored by the chat. The background video loads from the template’s existing Amazon CloudFront address; that request shares ordinary connection information, including your IP address, with its host. Chat messages and brief contents are not attached to video requests.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website visits and interactions. Google receives technical and usage information such as page URLs, browser and device details, and your IP address; Analytics may use cookies. We do not add chat messages, learner briefs, names or email addresses to analytics events. Learn more about Google’s privacy practices and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

The manual form

The manual brief is composed in your browser and downloaded only when you select Download. It is not submitted to Sanifu or saved by this form. Share it with David when you choose.

Calls and payments

Cal.com handles call booking. Direct booking links from this site include a Sanifu website source tag that Cal.com may store with a completed booking; it does not contain your chat or brief. When in-chat booking is connected, name, email and booking details are stored with your conversation; those structured fields are not sent to the model. Calendar deletion is separate from deleting a transcript. Stripe handles payment after you accept a written proposal. This website does not collect card details.

Questions

Contact David about your information. The website does not include advertising pixels or automatic newsletter enrollment.
