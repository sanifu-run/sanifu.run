Privacy at sanifu.run

Updated 29 October 2026. This page covers sanifu.run, which presents the Zati Agency workshops also published at zati.agency. Zati Agency is published by Sire Run, Inc. The notice on zati.agency describes that website. This notice describes this one.

What you can share

Use the page to ask about workshops and, if you choose, to draft a brief about your organization, website, and the process you want to improve. Please don’t include passwords, API keys, payment details, private customer records, or other sensitive information.

Prepared answers

While the page shows prepared answers, the questions you choose and the replies you see stay in the current page. They are not submitted, saved, or emailed. Reloading the page clears them. This is the current public state.

Connected chat, if it is shown

A connected assistant is used only after its answers match the published Zati offer. When that assistant is shown, messages and replies are sent to the configured service and can be saved for David to review. A random token in your browser can reopen the conversation. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to open it. Clearing browser storage removes the token, not a server copy.

When connected, saved AWS transcripts are set to expire 30 days after their last update. Expired chats are hidden by the application; DynamoDB deletes them later with its TTL process. Full transcript copies can be emailed to David after a chat has been idle and again when it changes. Those copies can include your IP address and browser request headers, such as browser type, language, and the referring page when supplied. Credentials, cookies, and gateway-internal headers are excluded. Deleting a chat on the website does not delete email copies or records kept by the inference provider. Do not submit sensitive information.

A connected chat may ask for your name and email so David can follow up about a workshop. Sharing is optional. It does not enroll you in a newsletter. Contact details you type are processed with the conversation. Your information is not sold.

Your brief

The manual brief is composed in your browser and downloaded only when you select Download. It is not submitted by this form. When a connected assistant is shown, you can review, edit, and approve a summary. Approval confirms that the summary matches what you meant. It does not book a call, accept a quote, or purchase a workshop.

Booking, email, and payment

The discovery-call button opens Calendly. Calendly receives what you submit there, under its own practices. Email to david@sire.run is handled by the email providers involved. This website does not take payment or collect card details. Paid work starts only after a written scope and quote.

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to understand visits. Google receives technical and usage information such as page URLs, browser and device details, and your IP address. Analytics may use cookies. Chat messages, briefs, names, and email addresses are not added to analytics events. Read Google’s privacy policy and the Analytics opt-out add-on.

Questions

Email privacy@sire.run or write to Sire Run, Inc., 584 Castro St Suite #4211, San Francisco, CA 94114, United States. Identify sanifu.run and the page or request involved, and avoid sending sensitive information. Business inquiries can go to david@sire.run.
